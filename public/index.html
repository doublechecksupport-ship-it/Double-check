About DoubleCheck

DoubleCheck was built to simplify complex legal documents, making them completely transparent, easy to understand, and accessible without expensive solicitor fees.

Whether you are an everyday consumer signing a residential housing lease, a gig worker reviewing an employment agreement, or a freelance creator handling client contracts and NDAs every week, legal jargon can easily catch you out.

DoubleCheck instantly extracts key payment terms, deadlines, hidden penalties, and risk clauses, saving you time and protecting your legal interests.
