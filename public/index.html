About DoubleCheck

DoubleCheck was built with a clear mission: to simplify complex legal documents, making them completely transparent, easy to understand, and accessible to everyone without expensive solicitor fees.

Whether you are an everyday consumer signing a residential housing lease, a gig worker reviewing an employment agreement, or a freelance creator handling client contracts and NDAs every week, legal jargon can easily catch you out. Hiring a lawyer for every single document review can cost hundreds of pounds.

By leveraging advanced artificial intelligence, DoubleCheck instantly extracts critical payment terms, key deadlines, hidden penalties, and risk clauses. We save you time, eliminate stress, protect you from unfavorable lock-ins, and ultimately save you significant money while maintaining exceptional analytical accuracy.
